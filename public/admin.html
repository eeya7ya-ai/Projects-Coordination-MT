How Email Notifications Work

  • When you create a new project and assign users, they automatically receive an email.
  • When you update a project and add new users, the newly added users receive an email.
  • Each email includes the project name, client, dates, priority, and module details.
  • The company logo (Magic Tech) is displayed at the top of every notification email.
  • SMTP credentials (SMTP_HOST, SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASSWORD) are set as Vercel environment variables.
